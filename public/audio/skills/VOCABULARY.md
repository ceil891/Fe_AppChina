Vocabulary pronunciation files are generated by scripts/generate-vocabulary-audio.py
using gTTS 2.5.4, Mandarin Chinese (zh-CN). They contain the 120 public starter
words listed in frontend/src/features/vocabulary/vocabularyAudio.json.

Playback serves local MP3 files; no learner text is sent for synthesis.
The manifest matches exact Hanzi rather than database IDs so edited words do
not accidentally play a previous word's audio. New words without a bundled
sample use an installed Chinese browser voice, with an explanatory message
when that voice is unavailable. Regenerate and review the manifest/audio when
extending the bundled vocabulary.
